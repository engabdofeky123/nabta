WHAT IS NABTA

One connected ecosystem for the modern farm.

Nabta is an end-to-end Agri-Tech platform built to support small and medium-sized farm operators through Precision Agriculture and Agriculture 4.0 technologies.

It combines real-time IoT sensor data, satellite imagery, Computer Vision, Machine Learning and conversational AI, then adds financial tracking, a direct marketplace and equipment rental. The goal: less wasted resource, earlier disease detection and better decisions at every step of the season.

COST & FEASIBILITY

Fewer sensors. More coverage.

Deploying sensors across every acre is expensive. Nabta divides each farm into agronomically similar zones, so one node can monitor roughly 1-3 acres depending on the zone.

Sensor readings act as ground truth, and satellite NDVI imagery with spatial interpolation extends that knowledge across the rest of the field, cutting the hardware a farm needs to buy.
